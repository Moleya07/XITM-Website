IT Security Policy Guidelines

These rules help protect the facility's computers, network and research information.

  • Each employee must use their own account and keep passwords private.
  • Use multi-factor authentication on sensitive systems where available.
  • Only authorised staff may use administrator accounts.
  • Do not connect unapproved computers, routers or wireless devices to the network.
  • Ask IT before installing software.
  • Keep approved software and operating systems updated.
  • Do not turn off antivirus or other security tools.
  • Report suspicious emails, links or attachments.
  • Store research information only in approved locations.
  • Do not send sensitive information to personal email or unapproved cloud storage.
  • Use USB drives only when they are approved.
  • Only authorised IT staff may change network device settings.
  • Changes to servers, switches and routers must be approved and recorded.
  • Report suspected malware, unauthorised access or data loss quickly.
Remember: Contact the service desk or Cybersecurity Officer if you suspect a security incident.
