Disaster Recovery Plans

This page gives recommended actions for problems that could affect the facility's network, servers or research information. Confirm which backup and recovery services are available before relying on them.

1. Network or VLAN failure

Record which users or buildings are affected. Check cables and switch ports, then ask the administrator to review VLAN and routing settings.

2. Server failure

Notify the service desk and administrator. Check the effect on DHCP, DNS and research-management services. Restore from a verified backup if available.

3. Cybersecurity incident

Contact the Cybersecurity Officer immediately. Follow containment instructions and keep relevant logs. Do not reconnect an affected device until approved.

4. Power failure or load shedding

Notify IT and facilities staff. Follow approved shutdown steps. Use UPS or backup power only if it has been installed and tested.

5. Fibre link failure

Identify which buildings have lost connection. Check link status and physical connections and ask the administrator to check routing.

6. Fire, flood or physical damage

Follow safety procedures first. Do not enter unsafe areas or switch on damaged equipment. Notify management and IT.

7. Data loss or corruption

Stop actions that may overwrite data and report the problem. Check available backups and verify restored information before use.

8. DHCP or DNS failure

Report affected users and services. The administrator checks server reachability, DHCP settings and DNS records, then tests the service.

General recovery steps

  • Decide which systems must be restored first.
  • Keep approved backups of important information and network configurations.
  • Test recovery procedures and record any problems.
  • The IT Manager coordinates recovery and updates management.
  • Agree on recovery time and data-loss targets before treating them as official targets.
