Purpose

This policy explains how employees should use the facility's computers, internet, email and other IT resources.

Acceptable Use Rules

  • IT equipment must be used for authorised work activities.
  • Employees must protect their login details.
  • Users must only access information they are allowed to view.
  • Computers must be locked when unattended.
  • Employees must use the internet responsibly.
  • Suspicious email links and attachments must be avoided.
  • Only approved software may be installed.
  • Personal devices require permission before connecting to the network.
  • Confidential research information must be protected.
  • Research information must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage.
  • Employees must not bypass network security controls.
  • Illegal downloads and pirated software are prohibited.
  • Unauthorised file-sharing applications are prohibited.
  • Employees must not access another person's account.
  • Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.
  • Suspected misuse must be reported to the IT department.

Monitoring

The facility may monitor organisational IT systems for security and operational reasons, in accordance with applicable laws and policies.

Employee Responsibility

Employees must read and understand this policy. Failure to follow it may result in appropriate disciplinary action.
