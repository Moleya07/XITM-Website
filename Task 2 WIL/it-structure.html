Roles and Responsibilities

IT Manager

  • Manages the IT department.
  • Approves major IT changes and purchases.
  • Ensures that IT policies are followed.
  • Reports to Senior Management.

Network and Systems Administrator

  • Manages the facility's servers and network services.
  • Configures VLANs, DHCP and DNS.
  • Maintains system backups.
  • Supervises the Network Technician.

Network Technician

  • Installs and maintains network cables.
  • Checks switches and routers.
  • Assists with fibre connection problems.
  • Reports to the Network and Systems Administrator.

Cybersecurity Officer

  • Monitors security threats.
  • Investigates cybersecurity incidents.
  • Protects confidential research information.
  • Reports to the IT Manager.

Service Desk Supervisor

  • Manages the IT support team.
  • Assigns and monitors support tickets.
  • Handles escalated support issues.
  • Reports to the IT Manager.

Service Desk Technicians

  • Help employees with computer problems.
  • Record support tickets.
  • Install approved software.
  • Escalate unresolved problems.
