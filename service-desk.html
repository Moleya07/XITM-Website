Service Desk / Help Desk Procedures

Staff should report IT problems using the approved service desk process.

  1. Contact the service desk using the approved reporting method.
  2. Give your name, department, contact details and the device or service affected.
  3. Explain when the problem started and include any error message.
  4. The technician creates a ticket for the issue.
  5. The technician checks the impact and urgency and assigns a priority.
  6. First-line support carries out basic checks that are safe and authorised.
  7. Network problems may be passed to the Network Technician or Administrator.
  8. Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Cybersecurity Officer immediately.
  9. The service desk gives updates where possible.
  10. The technician records the solution and confirms the service is working.

Ticket priority levels

PriorityMeaning
Priority 1 - CriticalA vital system or large part of the facility is unavailable.
Priority 2 - HighA major problem affects several users or an important function.
Priority 3 - MediumA single user or limited service is affected.
Priority 4 - LowA small issue or non-urgent request.
