Purpose

The Service Desk helps employees report computer and network problems and provides technical assistance.

Service Desk Procedure

  1. The employee reports the IT problem to the Service Desk.
  2. A Service Desk Technician records the issue in the ticketing system.
  3. The ticket includes the user's name, location and problem description.
  4. The technician identifies the type of problem.
  5. The ticket is assigned a priority level.
  6. The technician performs basic troubleshooting.
  7. Unresolved problems are escalated to the correct IT specialist.
  8. The user receives updates on the progress of the ticket.
  9. The technician checks that the problem has been resolved.
  10. The solution is recorded and the ticket is closed.

Ticket Priority Levels

P1 - Critical

A major failure affecting critical research services, servers or several departments. It requires immediate escalation.

P2 - High

A serious issue affecting an important department or business service.

P3 - Medium

A problem affecting one or a few employees where work may still continue.

P4 - Low

Minor problems and routine requests that do not interrupt important work.

Escalation

  • Network problems go to the Network Technician.
  • Server and DHCP/DNS problems go to the Network and Systems Administrator.
  • Security incidents go to the Cybersecurity Officer.
  • Major unresolved problems go to the IT Manager.

Ticket Closure

Before closing a ticket, the technician must confirm that the problem is resolved and record the steps used to fix it.
