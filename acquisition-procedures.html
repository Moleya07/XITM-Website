IT Acquisition Procedures and Approvals

Follow these steps before buying hardware, software or IT services for the facility.

  1. The department identifies the need and checks the budget with Finance.
  2. The requester submits a written request explaining why the item is needed.
  3. The request states who will use the item and what is required.
  4. IT checks whether an existing resource can meet the need.
  5. The Network and Systems Administrator checks compatibility with the network and servers.
  6. IT checks security, licences, updates and support.
  7. Supplier quotes are collected where procurement rules require them.
  8. Quotes are compared using price, quality, warranty, support and compatibility.
  9. Finance or Senior Management approves purchases outside the IT Manager's authority.
  10. Do not place an order before the required approval.
  11. Procurement places the approved order and the delivered items are checked.
  12. Add equipment to the asset register and give it an asset number.
  13. Configure and test equipment before using it on the production network.
  14. Keep approvals, supplier details, warranties and licence records.
