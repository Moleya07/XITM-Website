Purpose

These procedures explain how the facility requests and purchases new IT equipment, software and services.

Acquisition Steps

  1. The employee identifies the required IT equipment or software.
  2. A request is submitted to the department manager.
  3. The department manager checks whether the request is necessary.
  4. The IT department reviews technical requirements.
  5. The Cybersecurity Officer checks possible security risks.
  6. Software licensing and compatibility are checked.
  7. Supplier quotations are obtained.
  8. The IT Manager recommends a suitable option.
  9. The authorised financial manager approves the budget.
  10. Procurement places the order with the approved supplier.
  11. Delivered equipment is checked against the order.
  12. New equipment is recorded in the asset register.
  13. IT staff install and configure the equipment securely.
  14. The equipment is tested before being handed to the user.
  15. Purchase and installation records are stored for future reference.

Important Rules

  • Employees may not purchase IT equipment without approval.
  • All software must have valid licences.
  • Equipment must meet the facility's technical requirements.
  • Security requirements must be considered before purchasing.
  • Old equipment must be disposed of securely.
