Acceptable Use Policy

Facility IT resources should be used responsibly for approved work and research activities.

  • Use computers and the network mainly for approved work.
  • Only open files and systems you have permission to use.
  • Keep login details private and do not use another employee's account.
  • Do not try to bypass network security controls.
  • Do not download or share malware.
  • Do not install applications without IT approval.
  • Do not connect personal network equipment to the facility network.
  • Use personal devices only if the facility allows them.
  • Be careful with email attachments and links from unknown senders.
  • Report suspected phishing messages.
  • Do not upload confidential research information to unapproved websites.
  • Do not share confidential information with unauthorised people.
  • Lock your computer when you leave your desk.
  • Report lost or stolen equipment.
  • Use printers and shared resources responsibly.
  • Report unusual computer or network behaviour.
